Intelligent Autonomous Guided Car

An STM32 and OpenMV mobile robot integrating line following, obstacle navigation, visual localization, and wireless control.

Year2024
RoleTeam Lead · Team of Three
ToolsSTM32 · OpenMV · PID

Systems

  • Embedded C
  • OpenMV
  • PID Control
  • Bluetooth

One compact platform, multiple autonomous behaviors.

The project combined sensing, control, and embedded integration into a reliable vehicle capable of handling several navigation tasks.

System integration

As team lead, I coordinated the work of three students and integrated the STM32 motor-control layer with OpenMV vision processing, ranging sensors, and a Bluetooth interface.

Control

PID loops regulated steering and speed for line following. State logic connected visual localization and obstacle detection to different driving behaviors while keeping transitions predictable.

Result

The completed vehicle could follow marked routes, navigate around obstacles, respond to visual cues, and accept manual wireless commands—demonstrating a complete sensing-to-action embedded pipeline.
