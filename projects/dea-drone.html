Flight Control · Soft Robotics

Ultra-Lightweight DEA Drone Controller

A control and simulation stack for a 23-gram aerial robot powered by soft dielectric elastomer actuators.

Year2025
FocusNonlinear Flight Control
ToolsAdaptive PID · Sensor Fusion

Methods

  • Adaptive PID
  • Sensor Fusion
  • Nonlinear Dynamics
  • Simulation

Controlling flight with artificial muscles.

At this scale, actuator dynamics, payload limits, and noisy measurements make conventional multirotor assumptions difficult to apply directly.

Challenge

The 23-gram platform uses dielectric elastomer actuators rather than standard motors. Their nonlinear response and the drone’s low inertia demand a lightweight control architecture that can adapt as operating conditions change.

Controller design

I developed an adaptive PID structure around a nonlinear dynamic model and combined multiple sensor signals to estimate attitude reliably. The controller was designed to remain understandable and tunable while compensating for actuator variation.

Simulation workflow

A dedicated simulator reproduced the vehicle dynamics and actuator response, enabling safe parameter sweeps and disturbance tests before flight experiments. The result is a repeatable environment for refining the control strategy.
