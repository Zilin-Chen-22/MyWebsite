About me

Engineering intelligence
into physical systems.

I am a mechanical engineering student at Tsinghua University with a particular interest in how intelligent systems interact with the physical world.

My work spans robotic arm motion planning, autonomous flight, collaborative robots, embedded control, and simulation. I enjoy moving between mathematical models and physical prototypes—turning an idea into a system that can be tested, measured, and improved.
