Engineering intelligence
into physical systems.

I move between mathematical models and physical prototypes—turning an idea into a system that can be tested, measured, and improved.

I am a mechanical engineering student at Tsinghua University, working at the intersection of robotics, control, and programmable matter.

I am particularly interested in how intelligent systems interact with the physical world. My work spans robotic arm motion planning, autonomous flight, collaborative robots, embedded control, and simulation.

I enjoy research that crosses boundaries: theory informs implementation, simulation accelerates iteration, and experiments reveal what the model has missed.
