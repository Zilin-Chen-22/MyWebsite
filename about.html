About me

My previous projects covered robot modeling, motion planning, simulation, and hardware experiments.

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Hong Kong and a member of ArcLab, supervised by Peng Lu. I joined HKU in November 2026.

I graduated from Tsinghua University with a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering in June 2026 and spent an exchange semester at the University of Toronto. I previously worked as a decision and planning algorithm engineer at Beijing Keyi Technology Co., Ltd.

I have just started my PhD. My interests include autonomous UAVs and robot motion planning.
